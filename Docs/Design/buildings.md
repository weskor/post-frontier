# Buildings [New] — decided

> Part of the [Post-Frontier design](../Design.md). Related: [cards](cards.md), [commanders](commanders.md), [economy](economy.md), [fog](fog.md), [forces](forces.md), [units](units.md).

## Rules

- **Placement:** free grid placement in controlled, uncontested regions **[Built]**.
- **Limit:** production buildings are capped at **4 per commander** (solo 5), one per force ([forces.md](forces.md)) **[New]**. Everything else is limited by cost only **[Built]**.
- **Recycle [New]:** a finished building can be recycled for **50% of its build cost**. This undoes a wrong lock and frees the footprint.
- **Sharing [New]:** support, intel and logistics buildings benefit **the whole team**, so building one is a gift to your friends. Production buildings, their upgrades and their settings stay with the owner.
- **Start set [New]:** every commander starts a run with Barracks, Drill Rig and Workshop, plus 1–2 buildings of its own ([commanders.md](commanders.md)). Other buildings are drafted as Unlock cards ([cards.md](cards.md)).
- **Armor:** every building has the Structure armor class ([units.md](units.md)).
- **Structure HP is doubled** from today, so raids need commitment and Demolition units have a clear job: HQ 1800, Barracks 1000, Drill Rig 700, Workshop 800. New buildings start at similar values. Demolition keeps its ×1.5 bonus.

## Building list

Costs are starting values for tuning. Built costs are today's values.

| Category | Building | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Production | **Barracks** [Built, Change] | 220 | Brawler, Rifle, Scrambler, Repair crew |
| Production | **Factory** [New] | 280 | Artillery, Raider, Juggernaut |
| Production | **Lab** [New] | 300 | Lancer, Shield projector |
| Economy | **Drill Rig** [Built, Change] | 160 | Mines a deposit and pays into the team pool while connected ([economy.md](economy.md)) |
| Economy | **Overclocker** [Later] | 120 | The adjacent Drill Rig mines +50% faster and its deposit runs dry sooner. Cut from launch scope. |
| Tech | **Workshop** [Built, Change] | 190 | Required for **your own** tier-3 masteries. That's its only job at launch. **[Later]** Commander-wide defensive techs, cut because they are hidden modifiers that work against readable counters. |
| Defensive | **Turret** [New] | 120 | Kinetic gun. Tier 2 branches to Piercing or EMP, then a tier-3 mastery, like barracks. Medium strength: delays an attack and backs up a force holding the region, but loses to a full force or Artillery. |
| Defensive | **Barricade** [Later] | 40 | Cover for forces holding next to it; doesn't block paths. Cut from launch scope; region traits provide cover. |
| Support | **Repair Bay** [New] | 150 | Repairs units and buildings in a radius; pairs with the human repair twist |
| Support | **Shield Generator** [New] | 200 | Shields units and buildings in a radius |
| Intel | **Relay Tower** [New] | 150 | Makes regions in its radius fully visible, including unit types ([fog.md](fog.md)), and reveals JEV's next plan for them |
| Logistics | **Conduit** [New] | 100 | Bridges supply across one lost or neutral region ([economy.md](economy.md)); forward rally and refit point |

Each production building still locks to one of its units on first Start and grows through tiers and perks ([forces.md](forces.md)). Different units need different buildings, so *where* you build a Factory is part of your composition plan, and raiding one is a real blow.

## HQ

- **[Built]** Passive: no attack, 900 HP today, 1800 in the target (structure HP doubled); losing it loses the battle.
- **[New]** Two Failover Nodes guard each HQ, and a destroyed HQ goes offline for a hold rather than dying at once ([battle.md](battle.md)).
- **[Later]** HQ tiers and team calldowns. Cut from launch scope ([economy.md](economy.md)). The designed set, kept for later:

| Tier | Calldown | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emergency Repair | Repairs buildings in the region |
| 1 | Flare | Reveals JEV's next two plans and its composition in the region |
| 2 | Supply Drop | The region counts as connected for 60 s |
| 2 | EMP Burst | Strips all shields in the region |
| 3 | Artillery Strike | Damages everything hostile in the region after a 5 s warning |
| 3 | Rally | Every friendly force ordered to the region moves faster for 20 s |
