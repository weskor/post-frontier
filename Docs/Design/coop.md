# Co-op

> Part of the [Post-Frontier design](../Design.md). Related: [buildings](buildings.md), [commanders](commanders.md), [economy](economy.md), [forces](forces.md), [jev](jev.md), [open-questions](open-questions.md), [run](run.md).

- **Ownership [Built]:** only you command your buildings. There is no shared control.
- **Communication [New] — decided:**
  - Intent arrows for every commander's orders ([forces.md](forces.md)).
  - **Context ping:** one key pings whatever is under the cursor (a region, a JEV force, a building).
  - **Ping wheel:** *taking this*, *need help*, *JEV massing here*, *assault in 30 s*, *send Power*.
  - **Voiced callouts:** each commander voices pings in character.
  - Playtest with mics muted to prove comms work without voice.
- **Interdependence:** commander abilities and ultimates ([commanders.md](commanders.md)), shared support buildings ([buildings.md](buildings.md)) and gifting ([economy.md](economy.md)). **Missing:** JEV threats that need two commanders' abilities at once. None is designed yet ([open-questions.md](open-questions.md)).
- **Contribution card after each battle:** celebrates rescues, gifts and objectives. It never shames anyone.
- **Players leaving:** an AI adjutant takes over until the battle ends ([run.md](run.md)).
- **Solo:** a primary and a secondary commander ([commanders.md](commanders.md)); JEV is tuned separately ([jev.md](jev.md)).
