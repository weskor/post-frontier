# Meta progression: unlocks only [New] — decided

> Part of the [Post-Frontier design](../Design.md). Related: [commanders](commanders.md).

- **No permanent stat power.** Unlocks add *options*: commanders, cards, branches, JEV personalities, bosses, modifiers.
- **Unlock sources:**
  - **Score per commander**, earned win or lose, unlocks that commander's card bundles in a fixed order.
  - **Challenges** unlock specific items and teach behaviours, e.g. *win without Brawlers* or *gift 1000 Power in one run*.
- **Mixed lobbies:** a run uses the **union** of everyone's unlocks, so a new friend plays with the veteran's toys and is never held back.

## Pacing

| Runs | What unlocks |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Small curated pool; something unlocks every run, win or lose. All 4 commanders are available from run 1 ([commanders.md](commanders.md)). |
| 4–10 | Score-based card bundles per commander; challenge unlocks that teach |
| 10–20+ | The **Terms of Service** ladder, available after the first win: player-chosen modifiers. Late steps change JEV behaviour, not only numbers. Alternate bosses and 3rd branches come from ladder wins. |

## Terms of Service ladder

- Tracked **per player per commander**.
- The group votes on a run's level. It can be at most **the lowest member's unlocked level + 1**, so a group can stretch a little but never drags a newcomer far up.
- Ladder and challenge rewards include **cosmetics**: commander paint jobs, HQ banners and memo stamps. Status without power.

## Codex

A codex of discovered units, branches, cards, JEV personalities and **Machine memos**. The memos tell the story, and it advances after every battle and every lost life, e.g. *"We noticed you lost. Your feedback matters."*

## Weekly operation [Later]

After the launch content: a weekly seeded run with a fixed node graph, modifiers and commander pool, and scores normalised by party size.
