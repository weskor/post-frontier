# Construction, production and fronts

A new Boot match has two HQs, three neutral resource sectors, 600 resources per player and no friendly forces or buildings. Completed barracks maintain one persistent, typed force each. First Start locks Frontline/Ranged/Siege with capacities 6/4/2; prices are 20/30/50 per unit and durations 10/3, 13/3, 20/3 seconds. Siege additionally costs 180 once to configure. No level upgrade or shared squad cap remains. Alive travellers occupy the producer's slots and physically join the moving force; casualties reopen those slots. The enemy uses the same costs and producer ownership. Source: `CommandGameMode.cpp`, `CommandGameState.cpp`, `CommandBuilding.cpp`, `CommandBuildingProduction.cpp`, `ArmyGroup.cpp`, `ArmyUnit.cpp`, `CommandPlayerController.cpp` and `CommandHUD.cpp`.

## Targeted native recipe, after a fresh package

Use guarded `launch`/`doctor`/`focus`/`capture` and inspected coordinates. The compact HUD has a top status island, bottom-left live minimap, persistent Construction button/vertical building choices and contextual building inspector. F4 hides contextual controls but Construction/minimap remain. Choose Barracks, inspect footprint/validity inside the HQ cyan radius and left-click valid ground. Successful placement automatically restores build choices; immediately choose another building to prove that return. Right-click/Escape cancels targeting; Construction also cancels and reopens choices. An unfinished building's cancel row shows its unbuilt refund.

Wait for completion, select a force type, then Start & Lock. Inspect permanent type, joined strength/capacity, travellers, vacancies, one unit's timer and actual status/remedy: shortfall waits for resources, full force waits for casualties, blocked deployment needs a clear exit, and explicit pause requires Resume. Pause preserves partial work and does not permit changing type. First Siege Start costs 180 once; further Starts/replacements never repeat that fee. Assign Secure/Defend/Fall Back on navigable ground and observe individual paid recruits walking to their moving force. Build a second barracks for another type/front; its ownership and capacity must remain independent. Minimap clicks pan only, even during targeting. Manual squad controls remain removed.

Assign a barracks Secure front inside a resource sector and observe capture. Bare capture gives no bonus or durable building territory. Place/complete an outpost on the friendly uncontested sector; inspect income and persistence after the front moves troops away. Destruction removes rights/income. A completed owned workshop offers Siege Optics, Field Repairs or Entrenched Frontline for 150 once per commander; the owned card locks remaining choices. Entrenched Frontline now protects stationary automatic Defend units, not a removed manual Hold command. Pair purchase evidence with the matching effect scenario.
