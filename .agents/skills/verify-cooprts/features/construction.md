# Construction, production and fronts

A new Boot match has two HQs, three neutral resource sectors, 600 resources per player and no friendly forces or buildings. Units and buildings are data assets (`Content/DA_MatchContent` lists three units and three buildings, `DA_Barracks`/`DA_Outpost`/`DA_Workshop` at indices 0/1/2); actors carry definition indices and tests read costs from those definitions rather than enum switches. Completed barracks maintain one persistent, typed force each. First Start locks Frontline/Ranged/Siege with capacities 6/4/2; prices are 20/30/50 per unit and durations 10/3, 13/3, 20/3 seconds. Siege additionally costs 180 once to configure. No level upgrade or shared squad cap remains. Alive travellers occupy the producer's slots and physically join the moving force; casualties reopen those slots. The enemy uses the same costs and producer ownership. Production status is the typed `EProductionState` from `Rules/ProductionPolicy.h`; only the HUD turns it into text. Source: `Rules/ProductionPolicy.cpp`, `Content/*Definition.h`, `CommandGameMode.cpp`, `CommandGameState.cpp`, `CommandBuilding.cpp`, `CommandBuildingProduction.cpp`, `ArmyGroup.cpp`, `ArmyUnit.cpp`, `CommandPlayerController.cpp` and `CommandHUD.cpp`.

## Targeted native recipe, after a fresh package

Use guarded `launch`/`doctor`/`focus`/`capture` and inspected coordinates. The compact HUD has a top status island, bottom-left live minimap, persistent Construction button/vertical building choices and contextual building inspector. F4 hides contextual controls but Construction/minimap remain. Choose Barracks, inspect footprint/validity inside the HQ cyan radius and left-click valid ground. Successful placement automatically restores build choices; immediately choose another building to prove that return. Right-click/Escape cancels targeting; Construction also cancels and reopens choices. An unfinished building's cancel row shows its unbuilt refund.

Wait for completion, select a force type, then Start & Lock. Inspect permanent type, joined strength/capacity, travellers, vacancies, one unit's timer and actual status/remedy: shortfall waits for resources, full force waits for casualties, blocked deployment needs a clear exit, and explicit pause requires Resume. Pause preserves partial work and does not permit changing type. First Siege Start costs 180 once; further Starts/replacements never repeat that fee. Assign Secure/Defend/Fall Back on navigable ground and observe individual paid recruits walking to their moving force. Build a second barracks for another type/front; its ownership and capacity must remain independent. Minimap clicks pan only, even during targeting. Manual squad controls remain removed.

Assign a barracks Secure front inside a resource sector and observe capture. Bare capture gives no bonus or durable building territory. Place/complete an outpost on the friendly uncontested sector; inspect income and persistence after the front moves troops away. Destruction removes rights/income. A completed owned workshop offers Siege Optics, Field Repairs or Entrenched Frontline for 150 once per commander; the owned card locks remaining choices. Entrenched Frontline now protects stationary automatic Defend units, not a removed manual Hold command. Pair purchase evidence with the matching effect scenario.

Retain only captures showing distinct transitions, plus actions/game logs and a concise `RESULTS.md`. An editor-only or headless success does not establish a readable native HUD, mouse hit testing, or package freshness. Never use fixed screen fractions as selectors; doctor reports the actual window and scale.

## Offscreen HUD rendering (no window)

`scripts/hud_capture.py --run <fresh dir> --mode packaged --res 1600x900 --res 1280x720 --res 1920x1080 --res 2560x1440` launches one owned Vulkan `-RenderOffScreen` listen host and checks that no compositor window maps. Actions use shared drawn geometry and controller clicks; minimap coordinates check camera-only focus across targeting/hidden/selection modes, with the expected camera position derived from the probe's `arenaHalfExtent`. States cover launch viewport, overview, placement/hidden deck/construction, type choice and locked partial pause, starvation/resume, paid travelling units, naturally full four-unit Ranged force, full pause, real casualty deficit, moving paid replacement/arrival, workshop/research, resolutions and victory. Fixtures isolate the enemy, fund/pause income, inflict actual lethal damage and shorten victory; fronts use owning-controller RPCs at offsets from the replicated HQ position. PlayerInput receives Escape/F4/Space and removed squad keys. This proves the exercised HUD/Enhanced Input path and offscreen rendering, not OS input.

`scripts/hud_capture.py --run <fresh dir> --mode packaged --quick <label>` (optionally one `--res WxH`) is the development check: boot the same offscreen host, capture `<label>-deck` with the fresh expanded deck, fund and place one barracks through the placement fixture, select it, wait for completion (`productionState` must read `Unconfigured`), capture `<label>-inspector`, confirm no compositor window and stop. It proves only that the deck and a completed barracks inspector render at one resolution; production fill, starvation, fronts, minimap dispatch, research, other resolutions and victory are the full run's evidence.
