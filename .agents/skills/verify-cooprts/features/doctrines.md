# Paid workshop specializations

`ACommandPlayerState::Doctrine` starts `None` in a fresh match. A completed, owned Workshop offers exactly one permanent **150-resource** purchase per commander through `ServerResearch(Building, Choice)`; the owner pays once, and invalid, duplicate, foreign-building or terminal requests reject. Restart returns `None`. This is not a free F1/F2/F3 controller choice: those keys are not bound or supported by current native drivers. Each selected effect applies to that commander's produced squads, including squads made afterward, not another player's units. A controlled completed-workshop fixture supplies purchases inside the effect automation tests; it is not evidence of native construction.

- **Siege Optics:** siege has 1.25x effective weapon range and 0.75x outgoing damage, including against HQ. Integer damage uses `base * 3 / 4` truncation. Observe actual extended-band shots and reduced HP loss, not just range getters.
- **Field Repairs:** living units regenerate 5 HP/sec after five uninterrupted stationary seconds without firing or taking damage; interruptions reset the interval. Healing caps at max HP; dead or terminal units do not heal.
- **Entrenched Frontline:** stationary frontline units on an automatic **Defend** front take `incoming * 3 / 4` damage. Traveling Defend units, Secure, Fall Back and internal manual Hold do not receive it. The removed human Hold command is not required.

## Live proof and scope

`regression --scenario construction` (`CoopRTS.Construction.Lifecycle`) checks a paid owner-scoped completed-workshop purchase, duplicate rejection and independent second owner. For changed effects, choose only the relevant `doctrine-siege`, `doctrine-repairs` or `doctrine-frontline` run in its own fresh world; `doctrine-restart` checks real HQ outcome, terminal purchase rejection, `None` reset and fresh paid selection. Names: `CoopRTS.Doctrine.SiegeOptics`, `CoopRTS.Doctrine.FieldRepairs`, `CoopRTS.Doctrine.EntrenchedFrontline`, `CoopRTS.Doctrine.Restart`. These scenarios create controlled armies and paid workshop fixtures; they assert numeric damage/HP/owner behavior, not a normal player's build-and-click route. For complete baseline cover all four effect/restart scenarios and `construction` once, plus distinct affected production/combat/match paths. Require the exact Success and SoftQuit markers, exit zero and fresh per-scenario evidence. No doctrine effect pass should be inferred from the lifecycle/production results alone.

## Native purchase and effect

With a **fresh current package**, inspect empty starting base and wallet; use an inspected HUD WORKSHOP card, place in valid HQ territory and wait for construction to complete. Click the owned workshop body, then its actual SIEGE OPTICS / FIELD REPAIRS / ENTRENCHED FRONTLINE research card at the current viewport's scaled geometry. Capture before/after wallet and displayed specialization; a one-time choice marks that card OWNED and locks the others. Build a barracks and produce an applicable squad to inspect one changed effect in an encounter; HP/range numeric invariants remain live-assertion claims. If the desired role/encounter is not reached, mark native effect unverified, not inferred from a successful purchase. Check a different specialization only in a separate fresh match or after an actual terminal restart. `verify.py key` and `network_desktop.py key` currently have no `f1`/`f2`/`f3` choices; use guarded HUD clicks, not stale key examples.
