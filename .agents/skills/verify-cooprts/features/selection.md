# Building selection and HUD access

A fresh Boot starts at the friendly HQ with no player squads/buildings. Left-click an owned building or an owned living unit with a living producer to expose that building's controls; the unit is not individually commanded. Clear ground, foreign/enemy/dead units and orphan survivors clear selection. Orphans report “Barracks destroyed; survivors keep their last front.” Producer numbers use the lowest unused positive value per commander (one enemy scope); non-producers remain 0. A number remains reserved while a living producer or any group with at least one living unit holds it, including orphan survivors; it becomes reusable only after both are gone. Building labels/inspector and living unit labels show the number. Selection links the building to its force centre and that centre to a configured front. Tab/Q/H/R have no squad-control bindings. Space focuses the selected building or friendly HQ. F4 hides/shows contextual controls, but Construction and the minimap remain accessible. Accepted placement restores building choices automatically. Drawing, panel blocking and clicks share one layout in `CommandHUD.cpp`.

Force-number presentation uses the HUD canvas: bold commander-colour numbers on dark backed badges, one per friendly (team 0) living unit (including travellers/orphans) and living producer, including teammates. Each badge uses its owning commander's colour; enemy badges are excluded. Building badges and inspector numbers match the force snapshot while its producer lives. Projection skips behind-camera/off-screen badges; modal screens hide them and HUD panels cover them. Unit numbers no longer depend on `DrawDebugString`. Inspect badge readability at native scale, not only a magnified crop.

World squares, poles and force links are depth-tested pooled instanced geometry in both Development and Shipping, separate from canvas badges. Capture the cyan selected-building outline (+30 cm per footprint side, ground +10 cm, 3 cm width), cyan 150×150 cm front cursor (ground +13 cm), and each configured front's 150×150 cm base (ground +16 cm) plus 250 cm pole: Secure red, Defend green, Fall Back yellow. With a living force, capture the cyan barracks→force link and order-coloured force→front link (2 cm width, endpoints +24 cm); clear/cancel selection and confirm the corresponding geometry disappears. Repeat on a remote client and after restart; Shipping captures are required because Development alone cannot prove the debug-drawing cutover.

Recorded minimum: `Saved/Verification/round2/RESULTS.md` inspected native Shipping `barracks-selected-window.png`, `front-cursor-window.png`, `links-settled-window.png` and `defense-front-window.png` under `Saved/Verification/round2-shipping/`: producer/unit badge 1, selection/front squares, Secure/Defend poles and producer→force→front links. World force-identity and remote production-number assertions passed separately. Teammate colours, travelling/orphan badge cleanup, Fall Back, remote overlays, restart lifecycle, modal hiding and the full resolution/zoom matrix remain unverified; the source contract above is not proof of those surfaces.

## Targeted native recipe

Inspect a fresh current package, choose Barracks, click valid ground and confirm construction choices return without F4 or selecting the new building. Choose a second building immediately, then cancel. Hide the deck and click the persistent Construction button to reopen it. Select an owned building, inspect kind/force number/HP/status and its flat cyan footprint square (+30 cm per side, 10 cm above ground), and Space-focus it. Recruit from two owned barracks; compare numbered building/inspector/unit labels and click each force's unit to expose its own producer without moving the camera home. In a two-commander session inspect teammate building/unit badges in that commander's colour and confirm enemies have none. Inspect the building→force-centre→configured-front links; front targeting is a 150×150 cm square. A configured front has a 250 cm vertical flag pole and a flat 150×150 cm square base, red for Secure, green for Defend and yellow for Fall Back; the sector capture ring remains. Enemy/foreign/dead-unit clicks must clear selection. After producer destruction, click an orphan: selection clears with the destroyed-barracks feedback and its number/last front persist; a replacement must use another number and cannot adopt/select through that orphan. Once all orphan units die and no living producer holds their number, it is reusable. Clear on non-HUD ground and Space-focus HQ. Tab/Q/H/R must not select or manually order squads. Minimap clicks must pan without clearing selection, placing buildings or assigning fronts, including while a targeting mode is active.

Do not assume a fixed Army 1/Army 2, six starting roles, preselected unit or `key 1`/`key 2`. Those keys have no current controller binding and neither native driver accepts them. Old captures of Army 2's separate home and empty-group rebuild are historical; wiped groups are not revived with N. Avoid clicking HUD panels while attempting a world-selection/clear probe: panel clicks dispatch HUD actions instead.
