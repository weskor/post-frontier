# Capture, territory and construction economy

Each fresh commander begins with 600 resources and baseline +10/sec; the enemy has its own budget earning +10/sec per human commander (counting at least one) plus an unscaled +6/sec per established enemy sector. Resource sectors start neutral: three on Boot, eight on Availability Zone. Occupying a sector with living units can capture it, but **bare capture does not add income or durable build rights**. A paid, completed outpost in an owned uncontested sector establishes it: each friendly wallet then gains +6/sec per established site and barracks/workshops may be placed in that territory. The outpost persists without babysitting by troops; destruction removes its establishment/income/build rights. Buildings cost barracks 220, outpost 160 and workshop 190. Each barracks locks one typed force on first Start: capacities 6/4/2, individual prices 20/30/50 for Frontline/Ranged/Siege; Siege also charges 180 once for configuration. Replacements charge the same per-unit price on successful deployment, not a shared squad slot or manual purchase. Workshop specialization costs 150 once per owner. `CapturePoint.cpp`, `CommandGameState.cpp`, `CommandBuilding.cpp`, `CommandBuildingProduction.cpp` and `CommandPlayerState.cpp` own these rules.

## Primary live and network selection

`regression --scenario construction` (`CoopRTS.Construction.Lifecycle`) proves paid placement/rejection/refunds, owner isolation, locked configuration, real capture, bare-capture zero bonus, completed outpost, no-occupant persistence, an income tick and destruction removing establishment; costs come from the building definitions in `DA_MatchContent`, and the outpost/hostile positions derive from the sector and HQ actors. Its second controller/PlayerState is **in the same world**, not independent replication. `production` checks real per-unit prices, automatic paid casualties, independent force capacities, starvation and pause; `strategy` checks the enemy wallet and expansion; `rules` checks the wallet/insufficient-resources precedence without a world. `network.py --scenario economy --mode editor --clients 1` observes, on fresh host+remote, natural capture without income, a paid outpost establishing the sector after departure, owner-scoped once-only research, HQ weapon damage and fixture victory; `ownership` covers the 220 charge and rejected placements. `--scenario construction` chains them with explicit budget/capture/income fixtures.

For native proof, build a barracks, enable paid production and assign its Secure front inside a resource sector. Observe actual occupant capture. Bare capture must leave site income bonus at zero. Place and complete an outpost in that friendly uncontested sector, compare wallet/income, then move the barracks front away and inspect continued establishment without occupants. Observe a real enemy attack for destruction; a screenshot alone cannot prove loss of building rights. Pair images with action/game logs, and use live/socket assertions for atomic server rejection and independent replica convergence.

`regression --scenario economy` / `CoopRTS.Economy.CaptureIncomeRecovery` and the N paid casualty-purchase workflow were removed. Older economy captures/assertions are historical evidence for an obsolete rule, not current verification.
