# Capture, income and recovery

## Primary proof and selection

Run `"$V" --run "$LOGIC_RUN" regression --scenario economy` against a freshly built editor module in a fresh standalone Boot world. Require exit zero and `Test Completed. Result={Success}` for `CoopRTS.Economy.CaptureIncomeRecovery` in `economy.log`. The live scenario observes authoritative capture contest and ownership transitions, both player wallets with zero-site baseline and team-site income, quoted cost/payment, exact missing role restoration, failed-purchase atomicity, valid-source restriction and rebuilding an empty owned group at base. It uses real actors/units and the actual authority purchase path; it does **not** establish network replication correctness or hostile-client rejection. `movement` retains independent crossing, rejected order, joining-path and all-member arrival assertions; run it when those paths change. The scenario is implemented but not verified until its exact run result is recorded.

## Short packaged input proof

After package freshness passes, use a **new** desktop `RUN`. `launch`, `doctor`, `capture baseline`, then `key 1` and inspect the selected army's six roles, wallet, baseline income, replacement quote and N eligibility at the home marker. Use current screenshots to find a resource site: issue a ground right-click toward it, inspect accepted Move and progress, wait for ownership/progress and increased income on the HUD. If a fixed defender contests a site, observe contested progress before claiming capture; never infer site ownership solely from a changed wallet. `key 2` selects the **same player's** second army and the same wallet; it cannot demonstrate two-player wallet separation. The live scenario adds a second independent player state to check team income without personal last-capture credit. Report elapsed time and observed packaged wallet values rather than predicting a precise tick from screenshots.

For purchase input, allow a selected owned army to lose a member in an actual encounter (Q over a visible hostile; inspect health and casualty). R toward its base and wait until the group's center is within the purchase radius; inspect the HUD's missing-role quote and source eligibility. `key n` once: inspect wallet deduction, restored role/count, feedback, preserved current order and newcomer travelling from the valid source. `key n` again at full composition: require rejection, no wallet deduction and unchanged order. A living group far from its base and away from a controlled forward site must instead show ineligible source; N there must not spend or change its order. If a full wipe occurs in the encounter, select that **empty** owned army with 1/2 and purchase at its base; inspect the six restored roles and full replacement cost. A full wipe is not guaranteed by a short scripted input sequence: if not observed, mark packaged rebuild untested; rely on the live scenario for exact recovery semantics. Never use a free seventh unit as a positive result.

Keep this targeted: one clear capture and purchase/rebuild transition, not an exhaustive combat or navigation replay. Inspect captures and `actions.jsonl`/`game.log` together. HUD values are presentation evidence, not proof that a rejected RPC preserved the authoritative wallet or that two networked clients replicate the same territory. Exact state and atomic failures come from the live scenario. Record contested capture, casualty, purchase, rebuild and source restriction actually witnessed separately; second-wallet payment is live-assertion-only here. Do not upgrade an unobserved step to verified.

## Contract and limitations

Resource sites add income to **each** friendly player's wallet while team-owned; baseline income remains with zero sites. The player's last captured site does not determine payments. N requests paid replacement of missing roles for the selected owned group; empty owned groups are selectable and rebuildable at their home base. Nonempty groups may purchase near their base or a team-owned forward site. Replacement cost is displayed before payment, and rejected requests leave wallet, members and order unchanged. `ACapturePoint`, `ACommandGameState`, `ACommandPlayerState` and `AArmyGroup` implement the authoritative rules; `CommandPlayerController.cpp` routes the owned purchase request and `CommandHUD.cpp` presents current state. Capture at a fixed defensive position may require moving an opponent in a live assertion; this recipe does not claim the fixed defender is a strategic enemy commander.
