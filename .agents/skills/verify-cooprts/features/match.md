# Enemy strategy, headquarters and match loop

## Primary live proof

Run `"$V" --run "$LOGIC_RUN" regression --scenario strategy`, then `match-win` and `match-loss` in **separate fresh** `LOGIC_RUN` directories against the current editor module. Require exit zero and the named `Test Completed. Result={Success}` in each scenario log. `strategy` must observe the autonomous enemy choose and execute capture/contest, retain a non-emergency plan without replacing the order on every evaluation, interrupt for threatened-HQ defense, retreat while badly damaged, reach a valid reinforcement source and spend its wallet on exactly missing roles, then select an HQ attack when advantage permits. Scenario setup may control locations/health, but may not inject plans or directly set outcomes. `match-win` and `match-loss` must each reach a terminal result through living units' weapons damaging the relevant HQ, show one stable result, reject terminal combat/economy commands and verify a fresh restarted map with default wallets, rosters, sites, HQs and ongoing result. Standalone authority does not prove two-client replication or hostile-client security.

For a complete milestone baseline also run `orders`, `movement`, `combat` and `economy`, once each in their own fresh world. Those scenarios explicitly remove the autonomous commander from their test world, preserving their existing order/movement/combat/economy assertions rather than adding a product-level disable flag.

## Packaged proof recipe

Build/package current gameplay/content first; use a fresh desktop `RUN`, `launch`, `doctor`, `capture baseline` and inspect the **actual** screenshot. Press `key 1`/`key 2` and Space to inspect both friendly rosters and HQ health. At 1600×900 the plan/rationale panel begins near x12 y485–589, the N quote remains near y135 and site rows near y336–423; treat these as visual landmarks, never fixed click targets. Observe enemy-plan text and rationale while it moves toward and claims/contests a site; compare site ownership/progress and army positions at two times, not just the selected-plan label. If the chosen session does not naturally reach retreat, defense or HQ attack, record that surface as unverified; the live strategy scenario is the primary assertion layer.

For an HQ assault, identify the hostile HQ body in the current capture, position the selected friendly army within a useful approach, `point --x <fraction> --y <fraction>` at the HQ and `key q`. Inspect targeted Attack feedback, red target marker, falling HQ health and visible weapon attacks; an accepted order alone is not damage. Before terminal, point at a static-mesh **arena floor** hit and press Q: inspect `ATTACK AREA` with a null target. Point at the **friendly HQ** and press Q: require rejection with unchanged order serial/target, not a ground Attack at its base. Correlate these hit tests with `LogArmyOrders` and the live rejection assertions. After Victory, inspect the top-right overlay and HQ health; attempt Q on a living hostile or HQ, right-click on verified ground, H, R and N, checking order serial/units/wallet/health remain unchanged. Pan/drag/zoom and 1/2 selection, including a wiped group's hotkey if present, must still work after terminal. Press `key enter` once; capture fresh HQ health, six-unit rosters, neutral sites, opening wallet, reselected local group and no terminal overlay. A Defeat desktop observation requires its own fresh match and naturally lethal enemy weapons against the friendly HQ; do not claim it based on a test-only setup or a screenshot of Victory. If a desktop loss is impractical, retain the live `match-loss` proof and name the packaged-input gap.

Record exact scenario commands/results and evidence paths in `RESULTS.md`. Distinguish authoritative live assertions, observed packaged input/rendering and unverified multiplayer. A result setter call, direct HQ `ReceiveAttack` lethal shortcut, plan injection or resetting the existing world in-place is not proof of the required complete match loop.
